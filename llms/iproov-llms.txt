# iProov

> iProov provides biometric face verification and liveness detection technology used by governments, banks, and enterprises to verify that an online user is the right person, a real person, and authenticating right now. Core products are the Biometric Solutions Suite (remote onboarding and authentication) and the Workforce Solutions Suite (workforce identity protection against deepfakes and AI-driven impersonation). Founded in 2011 by Andrew Bud. Headquartered in London, UK.

Key differentiators: patented Flashmark technology for genuine presence verification, injection attack detection (beyond presentation attack detection), cloud-based Security Operations Center (iSOC) for real-time threat monitoring. First biometrics vendor certified to NIST SP 800-63-4 and CEN/TS 18099 Ingenium Level 4.

- [Biometric Authentication](https://www.iproov.com/biometric-authentication-solution): Face authentication replacing passwords and OTPs
- [Remote Onboarding](https://www.iproov.com/biometric-remote-onboarding-solution): Identity verification linking selfie to government-issued ID
